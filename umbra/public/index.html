UMBRA

A co-op puzzle played with real shadows. One phone watches the projected picture; everyone else steers from their own phone.

Host a gamethe phone that is wired to the projector
Try the simulatorno projector or camera needed
  1. Project the host page onto a wall (cast or cable). The host's camera watches it.
  2. Shadows are solid. Hold hands, books or cardboard in the beam to build ramps, bridges and walls for the orb.
  3. Phones nudge the orb, flip gravity and throw switches. Talk to each other!
