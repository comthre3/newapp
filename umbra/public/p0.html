PROJECTOR TEST

A 10-minute check that real shadows work in your setup. It measures delay, phantom shadows, shadow-fitting accuracy and connectivity, then gives you a report to send back.

  1. Point the projector at a bedsheet, wall or tent. Dim the room if you can.
  2. Aim the camera so the whole bright picture is in view, steady, off to one side.
  3. Start the camera (the page goes fullscreen so it is the whole picture), calibrate, then run the guided test. Follow the big text on the wall.

Needs https:// or http://localhost for the camera. Details: docs/P0_TEST.md
